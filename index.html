How to use it

  • One hour a day at most. You can learn today and apply tomorrow.
  • No fixed day for each lesson. Go in order; your next lesson always shows at the top.
  • If you miss a day, just continue from where you stopped. Don't do two lessons at the cost of your sleep.
  • After each lesson, write one line in "What did I learn?". Writing it in English is practice too.

End of the month: review with numbers

  1. For how many accounts do you now know the break-even ROAS?
  2. How many tests or offers did you suggest that actually went live?
  3. How many videos did you shoot, or briefs did you write, yourself?
  4. Do clients or Mohaned now ask you about the business, not only the ads?
